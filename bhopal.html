02 DECEMBER 1984

The Night of the Gas Leak

During the night of 2–3 December 1984, a toxic gas leak occurred at the Union Carbide pesticide plant in Bhopal, India.

Methyl Isocyanate (MIC) escaped from a storage tank and spread into nearby residential areas.

Thousands of people were affected. The disaster became a major turning point in discussions about industrial safety and chemical management.

01 / CHEMICAL SCIENCE

Methyl Isocyanate (MIC)

Methyl isocyanate is an industrial chemical used in the production of certain pesticides. It is highly hazardous, and exposure can cause serious harm.

The Bhopal disaster demonstrated the importance of safe chemical storage, industrial monitoring, and emergency preparedness.

02 / HUMAN IMPACT

The Human Cost

The gas leak affected a large number of people living near the industrial site. Many experienced serious health consequences, while families and communities faced long-term challenges.

03 / ENVIRONMENT

Environmental Impact

The disaster also raised serious concerns about industrial waste, chemical contamination, environmental monitoring, and the responsibility to protect nearby communities.

04 / LESSONS

What We Learned

Bhopal remains an important case study in industrial safety. It highlights the need for responsible chemical management, transparent risk communication, reliable safety systems, and effective emergency planning.
